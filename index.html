About Our Company

Your trusted business service partner in Tanzania

Eagles Professional Enterprises is a Tanzanian business enterprise committed to delivering reliable, efficient and professional services to financial institutions, corporate organisations, businesses and individual clients.


Our operations cover debt collection and recovery, auctioneering, digital auctioning and online asset disposal, clearing and forwarding, transport and logistics, car parking services, and the supply of building materials and hardware.


We aim to build lasting business relationships through professionalism, accountability, integrity and quality service.
