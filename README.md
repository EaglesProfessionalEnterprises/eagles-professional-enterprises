# Eagles Professional Enterprises Website

Responsive multi-page website for Eagles Professional Enterprises.

## What's improved
- Stronger corporate visual design using the existing navy, blue and red brand palette.
- Original company logo retained at `assets/eagles-logo.jpg`.
- Image-led homepage service cards and photographic service/gallery sections.
- Responsive navigation and layouts for phones, tablets and desktop screens.
- Existing company pages, service descriptions and contact details retained.

## Pages
Home, About Us, Why Choose Us, Debt Collection, Auctioneering, Building Materials & Hardware, Clearing & Forwarding, Car Parking Services, Gallery and Contact.

## Publish
Upload the **contents** of this folder to the root of your hosting repository. The entry page is `index.html`.
For Render Static Site, use `.` as the publish directory and leave the build command blank.

## Notes
- The service photographs are illustrative images loaded from Unsplash and require an internet connection. They are not represented as photographs of completed company projects.
- The contact email button opens the visitor's email application. No server-side form processing is included.
- Existing contact details and company information have been preserved.
